About

A whole year to deliver to the world.

Recruit elves, reindeer, and dwarves, make presents, and deliver them to children all over the world. The in-game year starts in January. The first few months are for getting ready: train your team, grow materials, tune up the sleigh, and when the time comes, set off around the world.

Time in the game runs on its own clock. A week takes about a real day, a month about three days, and a full year roughly a month. There is no rushing it.

This isn’t a game you play for hours a day. Check in for a few minutes in the morning and at night, then come back tomorrow. The seasons change at that kind of pace.

Make it through December and a new year begins. You carry over part of your team and progress and start again from January. The longer you play, the farther your deliveries reach.

Train your team

Raise elves, reindeer, and dwarves one by one. What you teach whom shapes how your year runs.

Letters arrive

Letters come in from children around the world—heartwarming wishes, and some that will make you laugh. Keep the ones you read as cards.

Something new each month

Every month a different troublemaker shows up, and each showdown is its own minigame—twelve kinds over the course of a year.

Grow a Christmas tree

Every delivery adds an ornament. A year’s worth of work becomes one tree.
